Research integrity

What staged testing does—and does not—permit

Earlier operational feedback

Each three-month tranche reports signal frequency, fillability and cumulative economics.

Only negative early stopping

The app may reject early only when every stringent futility condition is met.

No early success claim

No quarter, however profitable, can unlock the sealed confirmation period.

No retuning

The signal, scenarios, gates, tranches and stopping rule are hard-coded and hash-audited.
